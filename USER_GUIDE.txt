1. HOW TO LOG IN
================================================================================

1. Open Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on your desktop or office laptop.
2. Go to the ERP website link provided by your factory administrator.
3. You will see the "Kamani Plastic Industries ERP Sign In" screen.
4. Type in your registered Email Address and Password.
5. Click "Sign In".
6. The system connects to the live database in real-time. Once authenticated,
   your plant workspace loads immediately.

TIP: Bookmark the page in your browser by pressing Ctrl + D on your keyboard
so you can open it with one click every morning.

2. UNDERSTANDING THE MAIN SCREEN (DASHBOARD)
================================================================================

The Dashboard is your factory command center. It gives you a real-time summary
of the entire plant without clicking into separate menus.

1. TOP 4 KPI CARDS:
   -----------------
   - RAW MATERIAL STOCK (IN FACTORY): Total kilograms (KG) of polymer granules,
     yarn, and masterbatch sitting in the factory warehouse ready for use,
     along with total valuation in Lakhs.
   - WITH JOB WORKERS: Material (in KG) currently at outside vendors (twisting,
     braiding) waiting to be processed and returned.
   - FINISHED GOODS STOCK: Total units (Coils/Bundles) of finished ropes in the
     warehouse, split into "Free Stock" and "Reserved Stock".
   - READY FOR DISPATCH: Total coils allocated to confirmed sales orders
     awaiting truck dispatch, with total sales value in Lakhs.
   * Quick Action: Clicking any card jumps directly to that department screen!

2. CONNECTED FACTORY LIFECYCLE STRIP:
   ------------------------------------
   Shows the live 6-stage material flow:
   [1. Raw Material] -> [2. At Job Work] -> [3. In Process] ->
   [4. Finished Stock] -> [5. Sales Pipeline] -> [6. Dispatches]

3. ACTIVE JOB WORK LIFECYCLE TABLE:
   ---------------------------------
   Lists external orders currently in progress with vendor name, material,
   quantity sent, quantity received back so far, and status (SENT, PARTIAL,
   COMPLETED, OVERDUE).

4. CHARTS:
   -------
   - Inventory Distribution Donut: Balance between Factory Raw Material,
     Finished Goods, and Outside Job Work.
   - Finished Goods Bar Chart: Shows top rope products with Free vs Reserved.

5. REORDER ALERTS BANNER:
   -----------------------
   A red/orange warning banner automatically flashes at the bottom if any
   essential raw material or rope SKU drops below its safe buffer level.

3. INVENTORY & STOCK HUB
================================================================================

Click "Inventory & Stock" on the left navigation bar.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Material Arrives at Factory Gate (Raw Material Inward / GRN)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHEN TO USE: Whenever a supplier delivery truck arrives with polymer granules,
raffia yarn, masterbatch, or filler.

WHAT IS A GRN? A Goods Receipt Note (GRN) is official proof that stock was
weighed, inspected, and added to the warehouse.

STEPS:
1. Click the "Raw Materials (RM)" tab.
2. Click the green button: "+ Inward RM (GRN)".
3. Fill in the popup form:
   - Date: Today's date (auto-filled).
   - GRN Number: Auto-generated (e.g. GRN-2026-0042).
   - Supplier Name: Select or type vendor (e.g. Reliance Industries, IOCL).
   - Challan / Invoice No.: Number printed on supplier delivery slip.
   - Item Name & Grade: e.g. "PP Granules", Grade "H110FU".
   - Gross Weight (KG): Total scale weight from weighbridge (including bags).
   - No. of Articles (Bags): Count of bags (e.g. 100).
   - Article Type: Packaging style (HDPE Bag, Bora, Paper Bag).
   - Article Tare Weight (KG): Weight of one empty bag (e.g. 0.120 KG or 1.2 KG).
   - Received By: Gatekeeper or store supervisor name.
   - Vehicle Number: Truck registration (e.g. GJ-03-BW-4412).
4. System Auto-Calculation:
   Total Tare Weight = No. of Articles x Weight per Bag
   Net Weight        = Gross Weight - Total Tare Weight
5. Click "Record Inward Slip".
   Factory stock immediately increases. Click the printer icon to print a copy.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. Issuing Raw Material to Machines (Factory Floor Extrusion)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHEN TO USE: When plant operators take bags of polymer granules from warehouse
to extruder machine hoppers.

STEPS:
1. Under "Raw Materials (RM)" tab, click purple button: "+ Issue to Factory".
2. Select raw material (e.g. PP Granules H110FU).
3. Enter Gross Weight (KG), Number of Bags, and Bag Tare Weight.
4. Select Machine/Plant Line (e.g. Extruder Line 1) and Issued By supervisor.
5. Click "Issue to Factory Floor".
   Net weight is instantly deducted from warehouse inventory and recorded.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. Reading the Raw Material Ledger & Registers
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Use the sub-buttons to switch views:
1. RM Stock Ledger: Current balance, rate, valuation, and stock status.
2. Inward Register (GRN): Full history of all supplier truck arrivals.
3. Issue to Factory Register: Record of all raw material given to machines.
4. Stock Reconciliation: Compares inward - issues - job work with ledger.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D. Finished Goods Stock (Coils & Ropes)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Click the "Finished Goods (FG)" tab.

UNDERSTANDING THE 3 STOCK COLUMNS:
- Physical Stock: Total coils sitting physically in warehouse.
- Reserved Stock: Coils locked for confirmed customer orders (DO NOT SELL!).
- Free Stock: Coils available to sell = Physical Stock - Reserved Stock.

ADDING FINISHED GOODS STOCK:
1. Click "+ Add Finished Goods Stock" (or click a product row).
2. Enter coils produced, weight per coil, and batch number.
3. Click "Save Stock".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E. Stock Movements Log (The Audit Trail)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Click the "Stock Movements Ledger" tab.
Shows an unchangeable audit log of every movement (GRN, Issue, Production,
Dispatch, Job Work) with date, reference voucher, item, and quantity.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F. Batch Traceability Explorer
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Click the "Batch Traceability Explorer" tab.
Type any Batch ID, Sales Order, or GRN Slip to view the complete history:
Supplier GRN -> Job Work Sent -> Return Received -> Finished Coil -> Customer Dispatch.
Used for answering customer quality complaints within seconds.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
G. Stock Reconciliation (Comparing Registers vs Physical Balances)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Go to Raw Materials -> "Stock Reconciliation".
Compares calculated balance vs ledger balance. Variance should be 0.
If variance exists, check for missed GRNs or unrecorded machine issues.

4. JOB WORK HUB (EXTERNAL PROCESSORS)
================================================================================

Click "Job Work Hub" on the left navigation bar.
Manages outside vendors who perform twisting, braiding, or cutting.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Sending Material Out to a Processor (Outward Challan)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHEN TO USE: Loading a vehicle with raw yarn/granules to send to outside vendor.

STEPS:
1. In Job Work Hub, click "+ Issue Outward Challan".
2. Fill in:
   - Party Name: Select vendor (e.g. Galaxy Enterprise, Ramjibhai).
   - Material & Grade: What you are sending (e.g. PP Danline Yarn 800D).
   - Process Type: Twisting, Braiding, Hank Winding, etc.
   - Gross Scale Weight (KG): Scale weight with bags.
   - Number of Boras / Bags: Count of packaging bags.
   - Burning Loss % (B.Loss): Allowed loss tolerance (e.g. 2.0%).
   - Returnable KG is auto-calculated:
     Returnable KG = Net Weight - (Net Weight x B.Loss % / 100)
   - Vehicle Number, Driver, and Processing Rate (e.g. Rs 17.50 / KG).
3. Click "Generate Outward Challan".
4. Click "Print Challan" to print GST Rule 55 Delivery Challan (Original,
   Duplicate, Triplicate). Never let vehicle leave without printed copies!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. Receiving Processed Goods Back (Inward Receipt / GRN)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHEN TO USE: When job worker truck returns with finished coils or twisted yarn.

STEPS:
1. In Job Work Hub -> Orders, find the outward challan row.
2. Click the green "Receive Inward" button.
3. Enter Gross Weight (KG) and packaging tare rows (Bora, Cones, Theli).
4. Enter Accepted Net Quantity (KG), Rework (KG), Rejection (KG), Scrap (KG).
5. System calculates remaining pending balance:
   Remaining Balance KG = Original Sent KG - Total Received KG
6. Click "Confirm Inward Receipt".
   Vendor balance reduces and Finished Goods stock automatically increases!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. Extra Cutting & Scrap Deductions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHEN TO USE: When vendor returns material with trimmings, fluff, or end-cuts.
1. Go to Job Work Hub -> "Extra Cutting" tab.
2. Click "+ Add Extra Cutting".
3. Select Party Name, enter deduction details, Weight (KG), and Rate (Rs/KG).
4. Total deduction penalty is auto-calculated and added to vendor statement.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D. Vendor Statement, Ledger & Bora Account
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Click "Job Worker Statement & Ledger" tab.
Select vendor name and date range to see:
- Material Sent vs Material Received
- Burning Loss allowed vs actual
- Bora/Bag Account (Bags sent vs bags returned)
- Extra cutting deductions
- Net processing charges payable
- Click "Export CSV" to download an Excel sheet for payment clearance.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E. Printing Job Work Delivery Challans (GST Rule 55)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Click the printer icon on any challan row to generate standard legal Rule 55
document with GSTIN, HSN codes, gross/net weight, and transport declaration.

5. SALES & DISPATCH HUB
================================================================================

Click "Sales & Orders" on the left navigation bar.
Manages customer orders, stock reservations, truck loading, and billing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Booking a New Customer Sales Order
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHEN TO USE: Customer confirms an order by phone, WhatsApp, or purchase order.

STEPS:
1. Click "+ New Sales Order".
2. Select Customer Name, enter Customer PO Number, Delivery Date, Credit Terms.
3. Add Product Lines:
   - Select Product SKU (e.g. Danline Rope 6mm Yellow).
   - Enter Quantity (Coils) and Rate (Rs per Coil).
   - Click "+ Add Line" if customer is ordering multiple products.
4. System calculates Taxable Amount, GST (18%), Freight, and Grand Total.
5. Click "Save Order". Status will show PENDING.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. Checking Orders & Reserving Stock
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the order from the list to view Order Details.
2. Check the Free Stock column.
3. If stock is available, click "Reserve Stock".
   Status becomes RESERVED. These coils are locked and cannot be sold to others.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. Truck Loading & Generating Dispatch Challan
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHEN TO USE: Customer truck is at the factory loading dock.

STEPS:
1. From Order Details, click "Create Dispatch" (or "+ Dispatch Order").
2. Select the sales order.
3. Enter Dispatched Quantity (Coils actually loaded).
   * Partial Delivery: If order is 500 coils but truck only fits 300 coils,
     enter 300. The order keeps 200 coils balance for the next truck!
4. Enter Vehicle Number, Driver Name, Mobile, and Freight (Rs).
5. Click "Confirm Dispatch".
   Finished goods inventory decreases and dispatch voucher is created.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D. Printing Tax Invoices & Delivery Slips
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
From dispatch record or order details:
- Click "Print Tax Invoice": Full GST invoice with CGST/SGST/IGST breakdown.
- Click "Print Delivery Challan": Warehouse dispatch slip for driver.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E. Tracking Dispatches & Delivery History
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Click the "Dispatches" tab to see all historical shipments. When delivery is
confirmed by customer, update status to DELIVERED.

6. DIRECTORY & MASTER SETTINGS
================================================================================

Click "Directory & Masters" on the left navigation bar.
Master data configuration area for fast and error-free daily operation.

A. ADDING JOB WORK VENDORS:
   Go to "Vendors" -> "+ Add Vendor". Enter company name, phone, GSTIN,
   process type, and standard rate per KG.

B. ADDING CUSTOMERS (BUYERS):
   Go to "Customers" -> "+ Add Customer". Enter company name, phone, GSTIN,
   address, and credit terms.

C. SETTING UP JOB WORK RATES:
   Go to "JW Rate Master" -> "+ Add Rate". Pair Vendor + Process + Material
   with Rate (Rs/KG). Pre-fills automatically on all challans!

D. SETTING STANDARD TARE WEIGHTS:
   Go to "Tare Standards". Review standard empty bag weights:
   - Bora: 0.200 KG
   - Plastic Cone: 0.025 KG
   - Khali Bag: 0.120 KG
   - Theli: 0.015 KG
   Edit and save if packaging specifications change.

E. ADDING STAFF & SUPERVISOR NAMES:
   Go to "Personnel" -> Add supervisor and gatekeeper names. These appear in
   all "Issued By" and "Received By" dropdown lists.

F. UPDATING COMPANY PROFILE:
   Go to "Company Profile". Enter factory name, registered address, GSTIN,
   PAN, and phone. Appears automatically on all printed challans and invoices.

7. HOW TO EXPORT ANY REPORT TO EXCEL (CSV)
================================================================================

1. Look for the "Export CSV" or download button at top right of any table.
2. Filter the table to the data you want (e.g. date range or specific vendor).
3. Click "Export CSV".
4. The spreadsheet file (.csv) downloads immediately.
5. Open in Microsoft Excel, WPS Office, or Google Sheets to print or email.

8. STATUS BADGES & COLORS EXPLAINED
================================================================================

[GREEN]  IN STOCK    - Healthy stock level in warehouse.
[GREEN]  COMPLETED   - Order fully received or dispatched.
[GREEN]  DELIVERED   - Shipment successfully reached customer.
[BLUE]   SENT        - Material out with external vendor.
[BLUE]   DISPATCHED  - Delivery truck has left the factory gate.
[AMBER]  PARTIAL     - Part of the material has returned; balance pending.
[AMBER]  RESERVED    - Stock locked for specific customer order.
[ORANGE] PENDING     - Order booked, awaiting stock or dispatch.
[RED]    LOW STOCK   - Stock below safety buffer; re-order required!
[RED]    OUT OF STOCK- Zero stock remaining; stop issuing immediately.
[RED]    OVERDUE     - Vendor has exceeded promised return delivery date!

10. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON MISTAKE FIXES
================================================================================

Q1: Finished goods shows 0 coils, but coils are sitting in warehouse!
A1: Those coils are probably marked as "Reserved" on an open sales order.
    Check open orders in Sales & Orders. If order was cancelled or changed,
    update or delete line items to release coils back to Free Stock.

Q2: System calculated a negative net weight on GRN!
A2: Check Gross Weight and Tare Weight units. Gross weight must be in KG
    (e.g. 5000 KG), and bag tare must be in decimals (e.g. 0.120 KG, not 120 KG).

Q3: Cannot find vendor or customer in dropdown list!
A3: Party has not been added to directory yet. Go to Directory & Masters ->
    Vendors or Customers -> click "+ Add". Save, then return to your form.

Q4: Printed challan has incorrect address or GSTIN!
A4: Go to Directory & Masters -> Company Profile (or Vendors), correct details,
    click Save, and reprint.

Q5: Job work challan remains "SENT" after material came back!
A5: Store staff forgot to record the inward receipt. Go to Job Work Hub ->
    Orders -> find challan -> click green "Receive Inward" -> save.

11. FACTORY GOLDEN RULES (DO NOT BREAK)
================================================================================

1. RULE OF REAL-TIME ENTRY:
   Never keep paper chits for tomorrow. Record every GRN, issue, and dispatch
   at the exact moment scale readings are taken.

2. ALWAYS DEDUCT TARE WEIGHTS:
   Never enter scale gross weight as net weight. Always enter bag/bora count
   so tare weight is deducted accurately.

3. NO TRUCK LEAVES WITHOUT RULE 55 CHALLAN:
   Every vehicle carrying company material must carry an ERP-generated challan.
   Handwritten slips cause GST penalty issues during road transport inspection.

4. RESERVE BEFORE DISPATCH:
   Always reserve stock against an order before warehouse helpers load the truck.

5. PROTECT MASTER DATA:
   Never create duplicate entries for existing vendors or customers with
   spelling differences. Search first before adding a new party.
